# EP001 — Chintu Says Thank You to Food
- Track / age: Chhota Dharma (Sprouts), age 4–5 | Length: ~5 min | Value: Gratitude (kritajnata)
- Source: original habit story; grace-before-meals tradition (e.g. *Brahmarpanam* verse, Bhagavad Gita 4.24) simplified
- Characters: Chintu (little elephant), Amma-Tree (banyan narrator), Mother Elephant
- Palette: leaf green, warm cream, mango yellow

## Pause & think (5 s silence)
On-screen: "Who helped bring *your* food today?"

## Real-world action
"Today, before you eat, close your eyes and say thank you, softly, in your heart."

## Parent layer
Value: gratitude · Source: original story + grace tradition · Discuss: "Who do we thank for our food?" ·
