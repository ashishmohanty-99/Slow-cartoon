# 06 — Weekly rhythm + first 30 episodes

## Weekly rhythm (one value per week; titles are working names)
| Day | Format | Track |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Habit story (discipline/dinacharya) | Sprouts |
| Tue | Panchatantra/Hitopadesha fable | Sprouts/Saplings |
| Wed | Shloka & sound: "Little Mantra" with meaning | Seedlings |
| Thu | Epic episode (Ramayana/Mahabharata arc) | Saplings |
| Fri | Festival / "Why do we…?" explainer | Saplings/Young Trees |
| Sat | Nature, yoga & breathing (movement, not screen) | Seedlings/Sprouts |
| Sun | Big-kid story (Upanishad/Gita) + family question | Young Trees |
