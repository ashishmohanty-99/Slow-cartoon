# 02 — Curriculum by age

Guidance basis: AAP/WHO advise limiting under-5 screen time to ~1 hour/day of high-quality content,
co-viewed where possible, no screens for very young infants (sources in 05-resources). YouTube also
discourages targeting babies. So: **we do not produce for under-2s**; a small "co-view lullaby" playlist
is optional and parent-led.

| Track | Age | Length | Cuts/min | Voice | Teaches |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bala Bhajan** (Seedlings) | 2–3 | 3–5 min | ≤3 | Soft, sung/spoken, repetition | Sounds, colours, counting, one-line shlokas (Ganesha, Saraswati), gratitude to food/earth, bedtime calm |
| **Chhota Dharma** (Sprouts) | 4–5 | 5–7 min | ≤4 | Warm storyteller + 1–2 characters | Sharing, truth, listening, tidy-up, brushing/handwash routines, festival basics, simple Panchatantra fables |
| **Katha Kutir** (Saplings) | 6–7 | 7–10 min | ≤5 | Storyteller + character voices | Ramayana/Mahabharata child-sized episodes, discipline (dinacharya), anger control, respect for elders, yoga basics, nature |
| **Dharma Dhara** (Young Trees) | 8–10 | 10–14 min | ≤6 | Narrator + Q&A | Gita simplified (karma without fear), Upanishad stories (Nachiketa, Satyakama), focus/study habits, ahimsa, seva projects, "why do we do puja/aarti?" |

## Weekly values spine (all tracks)
Satya (truth) · Ahimsa (non-harm) · Seva (service) · Kritajnata (gratitude) · Shraddha (focus/faith) ·
Tapas (discipline) · Santosha (contentment) · Kshama (forgiveness) · Prakriti (respect for nature).
Rotate one value per week; each episode shows it in a small, doable behaviour.

## Every episode contains
1. 20-second calm open (character greeting + breathing cue).
2. Story / lesson (one idea only).
3. "Pause & think" beat (5 s silence + on-screen question).
4. Real-world action (e.g. "Today, say thank you before eating").
5. Close: soft "screen off" cue + end card. No "watch next!" push.

## Parent layer (description + pinned link)
Value of the week · source of the story · one discussion question · one offline activity · Sanskrit word with
simple meaning · printable (later).
