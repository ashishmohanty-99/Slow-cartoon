# 01 — Strategy

## Positioning
"The calm cartoon parents choose on purpose." Short, slow, gentle stories rooted in Sanatana Dharma
that end with a **real-world action** (breathe, tidy, thank, help) and a prompt to switch the screen off.

## Principles (non-negotiable)
1. **Learning tool, not hook.** No cliffhangers, no autoplay bait, no flashing, no loud stingers.
   Every episode ends with a "now go and…" activity and a calm end card.
2. **Parents are the customer.** Titles/descriptions speak to parents; each video has a one-line
   "talk about it together" question and a downloadable activity (pinned link/description).
3. **Authentic to the source.** Stories come from primary or well-known traditional sources
   (Panchatantra, Hitopadesha, Jataka-style fables, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Bhagavata, Upanishadic
   stories, festival legends, Bhagavad Gita simplified for 8–10). Every dharma episode lists its source.
4. **Inclusive, non-sectarian, no fear.** No hell/punishment imagery, no "other religions are wrong",
   no caste/gender stereotypes. Teach values (satya, ahimsa, seva, kritajnata, shraddha) first, deities and
   festivals as warm cultural stories.
5. **Humans stay in the loop.** AI may draft; a human (you) approves script, art and voice every episode.

## YouTube policy reality (verify at publish time)
- **Made for Kids (COPPA):** kids' content must be marked "made for kids". Consequences: comments, end
  screens, memberships and personalised ads are disabled, so ad revenue per view is much lower. Plan
  monetisation around parents (below), not CPM. Check the official pages: YouTube Help
  [support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076) and the
  Partner Program / "inauthentic content" policy.
- **"Inauthentic / mass-produced content" (clarified July 2026):** channels that publish near-identical,
  templated AI output can lose monetisation. Daily upload makes this the #1 risk. Mitigations:
  distinct stories, original script + art direction, recurring *named characters with personality*,
  varied formats across the week, human narration or heavily directed voice, visible editorial care.
- **Altered/synthetic content disclosure:** use YouTube's disclosure toggle when required (realistic
  synthetic voices/people). Stylised cartoons generally do not need it, but check the current rule.
- **Sources read during planning:** [TechTimes on AI kids cartoons & COPPA](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320340/20260713/ai-kids-cartoon-gold-rush-has-hidden-tax-coppa-cuts-revenue-80.htm),
  [Metricool on YouTube AI rules](https://metricool.com/youtube-ai-content-rules-monetization/),
  [TechCrunch, July 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/).
  These are secondary sources; the official YouTube pages win.

## Honest note on "daily"
Daily is achievable only with a pipeline that reuses *tooling* (not content). Start with 3–4/week for
the first 3 weeks while quality is tuned, then move to daily with a 10-episode buffer. A skipped day is
better than a weak episode — this channel's brand is trust.

## Revenue model (realistic)
1. Ads (reduced under Made-for-Kids) — small, treat as bonus.
2. **Parent-facing products:** printable activity packs, story books (PDF/print), habit charts, festival
   guides — sold via a separate website/Etsy/Gumroad linked from the description (parent-directed, not child-directed).
3. Sponsorships from values-aligned brands (books, Indian-culture education) — vet strictly; no toys/junk-food.
4. Optional: translated audio tracks (Hindi, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali…) to reach diaspora + Indian households.

## Channel architecture
- Main channel (English + Hindi audio track) → later parallel language channels or multi-audio.
- Playlists = age tracks + themes (see curriculum). Playlist-first navigation lets parents pick; avoid
  a single endless feed.
- Mascots: 2–3 recurring characters (e.g. *Chintu the little elephant* for habits, *Amma-Tree* the wise banyan
  narrator for stories, *Dhara* the girl-sage for older kids). Names are placeholders — owner decides.
